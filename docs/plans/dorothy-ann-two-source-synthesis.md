## Handoff

P2 is implemented: a shared provider-neutral floor counts unique source IDs with non-whitespace extracted text in final root knowledge (including admissible context evidence), normalizes the resolver outcome before SSE, guards the turn executor, and prevents direct `AnswerSynthesizer` provider calls below the floor. Existing bounded stops remain authoritative; one-source `resolved` becomes retryable `insufficient` with `no_new_knowledge`, retained evidence/closure and no synthesis. One-source `best_effort` retains its actual bounded stop reason but becomes retryable `insufficient`. Two conflicting sources still count; no citation quota or new search is imposed. The fixture research search now provides two distinct viable pages so normal fixture research remains answerable; explicit fixture `/link` search still provides its original single result.

This plan supersedes the earlier *prompt-only* root corroboration target for this particular product behavior, without editing the archived/done plans. The desired mechanical rule is **two distinct usable extracted source identities available to the final root synthesis**, whether they agree or disagree. It is not a two-citation quota or a test of publisher independence. The assessor still decides whether evidence addresses the question and preserves conflicts. P4 reconciled Jonny's pre-plan prompt edits: `ASSESSOR.md` now requests two distinct usable extracted source IDs at the root, not corroboration or a four-source quota; `SYNTHESIZER.md` reports agreement or disagreement faithfully and uses exact citation-marker syntax without a fixed citation quota. Output, trust, freshness, style, and obligation-coverage guidance were retained, and the stale exact-string prompt test was updated.

Current path: an exact-question Brave search requests up to five candidates; `EvidenceAcquirer` selects and extracts the first three with rank-layer fairness, then tries ranks four and five one at a time **only while fewer than two distinct viable root source IDs are available**. Each attempt—including empty/failed pages—consumes source fuel; work stops at five attempts/request or twelve/turn, while search/depth/assessment ceilings remain three/two/eight. Known viable context and earlier current-turn evidence count without re-extraction, and only viable nonempty text enters evidence/source closure. A root with fewer than two usable sources remains retryably insufficient without synthesis. `ResearchTimingCollector` emits opt-in aggregate search/yield counts, now including up to five charged selections per request; no queries, IDs, page data or provider payloads enter logs. The live marginal effect on `sufficient` outcomes is still unmeasured.

## Desired Outcome

```text
root question → search (≤5 candidates) → extract first 3 fairly
                                            └─ if <2 usable: try ranks 4–5, one charged attempt at a time
                     ↓
root assessment → ≥2 usable and answerable: synthesize once
                → otherwise: focused search / justified decomposition / retryable insufficient
```

A research answer is not synthesized unless its final root knowledge contains at least two distinct source IDs with viable extracted text available to that synthesis. The rule also applies to `best_effort` and follow-up answers; admitted usable context evidence may count, but bare search results, source metadata, snippets, failed extractions, and two snapshots of the same source ID do not. A pair may disagree. **Owner-confirmed answer behavior:** when sources corroborate a claim, summarize their agreement; when they conflict, present the competing accounts and what each source supports, without flattening the conflict into consensus or excluding a usable source solely because it disagrees. If the question is about what the evidence says, a faithful account of disagreement may satisfy it; if the question requires a definitive factual conclusion that the evidence cannot establish, state the uncertainty and use `best_effort` when at least two sources are available. Do not manufacture a second citation or force the answer to cite both if its claims do not use both; an answer's citation count and the number of sources presented to synthesis are distinct. Semantic relevance and obligation coverage remain assessor-judged, not inferred from two IDs alone. The user can ask a follow-up research question to investigate unresolved differences using admitted context evidence; the two-source gate does not initiate unbounded automatic investigation.

With fewer than two usable sources at a bounded stop, do **not** produce a one-source synthesized answer. Preserve the acquired evidence and terminal provenance through a truthful insufficient-evidence outcome (or an existing typed execution failure when that is the actual failure); permit a later retry. A root may still be `best_effort` with two sources if its obligations are not fully met. Do not force two subquestions or make child problems meet a two-source floor; a focused second search often suffices.

## Implementation Plan

### P1 — measure the evidence funnel before tuning it

Extend opt-in server-only, turn-local, allowlisted aggregate diagnostics (ideally the existing `research_timing` record, versioned if its shape changes) with bounded counts at each search/request and terminal boundary:

- candidate results requested and normalized/unique; candidates skipped for invalid/duplicate identity;
- new sources selected for extraction and candidates left unselected after the first three; extraction viable/nonempty versus failed/empty, with a short allowlisted failure category when reliably available;
- distinct viable evidence IDs available at root resolution/synthesis, and final outcome (`sufficient`, `best_effort`, `insufficient`); optionally distinct citation IDs as a **separate** metric.

Use no queries, URLs, source IDs, titles, extracted text, provider payloads, thread IDs, or credentials in logs. Keep logging opt-in; counters must not affect control flow or become durable/browser output. Do not count a provider call as evidence merely because the HTTP request succeeded. Validate fixture cases for zero results, duplicate/invalid results, failed/empty extraction, mixed success, reused context evidence, and one-source best effort. Confirm one record per turn and stable bounds.

Run a small, consented live/staging sample of representative simple, contested, and multi-obligation questions with the opt-in record. Compare how often the first request yields 0/1/2/3 viable sources, and partition `<2` cases into too few candidates, unselected candidates with extraction failures, and selected-but-failed extraction. Record only aggregate tallies in this plan; do not paste raw prompts or provider data. Metrics of *unselected* candidates do not prove they would extract successfully. Evaluate that counterfactual with deterministic fixtures or a separately approved bounded staging experiment, not silent extra live fetches.

### P2 — enforce the two-source synthesis gate

Add one provider-neutral application check on the final root knowledge's unique viable extracted source IDs before the single synthesis call, for both `sufficient` and `best_effort` outcomes. Prevent a false `sufficient` terminal when the gate fails; when further budget remains, prefer focused-search/reassessment behavior rather than rejecting after the resolver has already stopped. The observed second search returned no new usable evidence and currently stops with `no_new_knowledge` even though one search and three source selections remain. **P2 decision (owner approved gate first):** retain the current `no_new_knowledge` stop and all search budgets; return a retryable insufficient turn with its viable evidence when the two-source floor is unmet. Do not invent/repeat a query or consume remaining fuel merely to satisfy a source count. Evaluate any distinct-query continuation separately in P3. At budget/availability exhaustion, use the approved insufficient path with retained evidence. Reconcile root and child semantics, follow-up context, checkpoint/source closure, and existing terminal schemas. Keep search turns (`/link`, `/image`, `/video`) unchanged. No citation quota and no publisher-independence heuristic.

### P3 — tune acquisition only if P1 identifies a yield bottleneck

If low yield is driven by selected sources failing while unselected Brave candidates remain, propose deterministic backfill for failed/empty extraction within the same request; compare against better extraction behavior and a candidate-count change using fixtures and staging evidence. Explicitly settle whether a failed selection consumes the three-per-request ownership cap and nine-source budget **before** modifying the allocator: current rules count selected failed attempts and prohibit backfill. Preserve bounded work, deterministic ordering, fairness across requests, privacy, and provider-neutral ports; do not silently exceed existing budgets or add an unbounded retry loop. If search results rarely contain enough candidates, backfill is not the remedy; investigate search/query quality separately. If first-search yield is usually already ≥2, prioritize the synthesis gate and prompt alignment instead of an acquisition rewrite.

## P1 instrumentation checkpoint

The opt-in server-side `research_timing` record is now schema version 2. `RESEARCH_TIMING_LOGS=true` enables it (debug logging also enables the existing timing path); an injected server timing sink is available for tests. It contains up to three ordered per-search/request aggregate entries: `requested` (configured candidate ceiling), `returned` (provider-normalized array size, capped at five), `normalized_unique` (acquirer-admitted unique canonical URLs), `invalid_discarded`/`duplicate_discarded` (only safely visible at the acquirer boundary), `selected` (new owned extraction attempts, up to five after P3), `reused` (already-available/shared associated candidates), `unselected` (neither owned nor associated), `viable` (nonempty selected extraction), `empty`, `failed` with allowlisted `fetch_failed`/`timeout`/`extract_failed` subtotals, and `skipped_other`. `distinct_viable_root_ids` counts unique nonempty extracted source IDs in final bounded root resolution knowledge, including context evidence; it is not a citation count or relevance judgment. Existing `resolution_status` records final sufficient/best-effort/insufficient outcomes. An absent root count means no final resolution (e.g. checkpoint), not zero evidence. The record contains no source IDs or raw content and does not enter SSE or durable turns. A provider may filter invalid/duplicate raw Brave items before returning; these upstream discards cannot be measured by provider-neutral acquisition and therefore acquirer discard counters are lower bounds, not raw Brave-response diagnostics. Unselected candidates have not been extracted; they do not imply usable evidence. Counters are bounded and observer/sink errors are swallowed.

## P3 decision checkpoint — candidate yield versus extraction yield

The recent operator log repeats the earlier low-yield pattern: two searches each returned five unique candidates; six selected attempts produced one viable extracted page and five `empty_content` outcomes; four candidates were left unselected. With P2 active, the turn correctly ended retryable `insufficient` with no synthesis. This is a repeat of a similar question/pattern, **not** a new independent estimate of population frequency. A fresh one-off exact-query Brave call also returned five normalized candidates across three domains; it tested search metadata only, not the viability of previously unselected results, and cannot replay the original ranking. The separate 15-question post-schema-fix pilot reached at least two final usable sources in 15/15 turns, while the initial P1 pilot reached 14/15. Small selected samples cannot settle the tail risk.

Raising Brave's candidate count alone cannot help the observed runs: five candidates already arrived, and only three were selected. A better extractor may improve `empty_content` outcomes (the current Readability/plain-text extraction requires 120 characters), but the logs cannot distinguish short pages from JavaScript-only content or determine whether a different extraction strategy would succeed. Trying ranks four and five can rescue a sparse turn **only if** they extract viable text; their snippets are not evidence of that. Current `EvidenceAcquirer` counts each attempt, including `empty_content`/failure, against three owned attempts per request and nine consumed attempts per turn. A same-request replacement would change the former cap (e.g. up to five *attempts*, stopping on two viable pages) or change accounting to stop charging failed attempts; the latter would permit more work than the existing turn cap and is not silently acceptable. Even with the nine-attempt turn cap unchanged, allowing five attempts in one request reserves fewer for later requests/branches, so fair rank-layer ordering and budget availability must be explicit. These are contract decisions, not a logging tweak.

Jonny approved the read-only bounded staging counterfactual. It used exactly one fresh exact-question Brave search, which returned five normalized candidates; the current safe extractor fetched only ranks four and five. Rank four was viable, rank five returned `empty_content` (zero or under 120 readable characters). No URLs, titles, extracted text, or provider payloads were logged or retained in this plan. This shows a spare candidate *can* be viable for this fresh search; it does **not** establish whether ranks one to three were viable this time, that the historical rank-four page was the same, or that backfill would have saved the earlier turn. Local `.env` disabled TLS verification for this exploratory probe as previously authorized; this is not deployment acceptance.

**Approved P3 contract (Jonny):** prefer targeted *charged* backfill over increasing candidate count or waiving failed-attempt accounting. Select/extract the initial rank-layer wave of at most three candidates per request as before. When fewer than two distinct viable source IDs are available across current root evidence/context and that wave, try remaining candidates one at a time in deterministic rank-layer/request-priority order; recheck the shared two-source floor after each extraction. Allow at most **five owned attempts per request** (including failed/empty ones), **twelve charged attempts per turn**, five search candidates per request, and three concurrent initial extractions. Each backfill attempt consumes global source fuel; stop at the floor, candidate exhaustion, per-request cap, or global cap. No extra search, depth, assessment, fabricated evidence, or citation quota. Known viable context/current-turn evidence and shared candidates count toward the floor without re-extraction; canonical URL dedupe and fair ownership remain in force. If a request already has enough viable root evidence after its initial wave, no backfill. Extra early attempts can still leave fewer for later branches despite the raised turn cap. A counterfactual gain in `sufficient` outcomes remains unproven until post-change verification; do not silently add further retry/continuation behavior. Do not treat metadata or deterministic fixtures as proof those live spare pages are viable.

## Non-goals

- Logging private research inputs or raw provider data to diagnose yield.
